## What the reasoning rests on, from the record

**The dependence is not disputed and is not a matter of inference — the filer
states it and names the customer.** From the 10-K the intake fetched
(`0000772406-26-000018`, filed 2026-05-21), Item 1 *Customers* and again in
Item 1A:

```
For fiscal years 2026, 2025, and 2024, we had one end customer, Apple Inc.,
who purchased through multiple contract manufacturers and represented
approximately 91 percent, 89 percent, and 87 percent, of the Company's total
net sales, respectively.
```

**87 → 89 → 91 per cent. The concentration is rising, not falling.** The ten
largest end customers are 96%.

**And the filer itself names the two mechanisms the owner says he cannot
judge**, in the same risk factor:

```
- many of our customers have sufficient resources to internally develop
  technology solutions and semiconductor components that could replace the
  products that we currently supply in our customers' end products;

- our customers regularly evaluate alternative sources of supply in order to
  diversify their supplier base, which increases their negotiating leverage
  with us and their ability to either obtain or dual-source components from
  other suppliers;
```

and, on the pricing half of the owner's sentence:

```
In addition, our dependence on a limited number of key customers may make it
easier for them to demand favorable commercial terms or to pressure us on
price reductions... We have experienced pricing pressure from certain key
customers, and we expect that the average selling prices ("ASPs") for certain
of our products will decline from time to time, potentially reducing our
revenue, margins, and earnings.
```

**There is no contractual floor either.** Item 1, *Backlog*: *"Sales are made
primarily pursuant to short-term purchase orders... The quantity actually
ordered by the customer, as well as the shipment schedules, are frequently
revised, without significant penalty."*

**THE BRIEFING'S SEARCH ALREADY BEHAVED THE WAY THE OWNER'S ARGUMENT
PREDICTS.** `reports/BRIEFING-CRUS-2026-09-08.md` §8 records: *"Whether Apple
is taking the content in-house: NOT ESTABLISHED. The 10-K names the risk in
the abstract... and no 2026 reporting was found alleging it has happened."*
The owner's point is stronger than that finding and supersedes it: **the
search was not going to succeed in time whatever effort was spent on it.**
US patent applications publish eighteen months after filing, so a decision
already taken inside Apple is either invisible now or visible only after the
price has moved. **An absence of evidence here is not evidence of absence; it
is the expected reading of an unobservable.**

## Why this is the BETS-B.ST ground and not a verdict on the business

The owner names the precedent himself, and it is exact. BETS-B.ST was
dropped on 2026-08-25 with the reason recorded as *"the thesis rests on
whether that B2B loss has a floor, a question about Turkish enforcement
policy the owner cannot judge better than the market"* — and, in the same
row, *"The company is not financially impaired."*

**The same two halves apply here, and the second is the one a later reader
will need.** CRUS's own record from this intake is a company with **no
borrowings at all, $666,953,000 of net cash, an operating margin of 23.05%
and an operating profitability of 18.49%** — the last agreeing with the
screener's independently-computed 18.45% to four basis points. Nothing in the
accounts is impaired and nothing was found wrong with them: **the store
carries ZERO refusals**, which no other name intaken this week managed.

**The drop is a statement about the OWNER'S INFORMATION SET, not about the
company.** His own last sentence says so: *"Bolaget kan vara utmärkt — det är
frågan jag inte kan svara på."*

**NO RE-ENTRY CONDITION WAS STATED, and none is invented here.** The ADBE row
carries one because the owner gave one; this one does not because he did not.
What can be said as a fact rather than as a rule: the concentration figure is
an ANNUAL disclosure, restated once a year in the 10-K, and the next one is
due around May 2027.

## What stands as a record, and why it was kept

**A dropped name is a RECORD.** Everything the intake produced is left in
place, complete and dated, so that anyone picking CRUS up later starts from
the work rather than repeating it.

**1. The store — `config/manual/CRUS.yaml`.** 77 figures, basis **annual
FY2026 ending 2026-03-28**, **zero refusals**. Every tagged fact is VERIFIED
by provenance under E40 rule 1. It carries the CIK — 772406, looked up in
SEC's own `company_tickers.json` and confirmed against `data.sec.gov/
submissions`, sourced and dated on the field. It carries six hand-added
zeros, each resting on a sentence in the 10-K rather than on an absent tag:
no amounts outstanding under the revolver, a 401(k) with no defined-benefit

**2. The basis figures, computed and never used.** FCF0 **$577,295,000**; net
CASH **$666,953,000**; divisor **52,822,000**. E101's checks: check 3 inside

10-K states no free cash flow, no net debt, and the words "non-GAAP" do not
occur in it. That is an absence of a check and not a passed one, and it stays
recorded as such.

**3. The price work — and it is the part most likely to be re-derived

**4. The briefing — `reports/BRIEFING-CRUS-2026-09-08.md`.** E76's eight
