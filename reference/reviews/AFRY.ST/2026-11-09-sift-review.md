## What it sells (Q5)

Engineering and design consulting -- energy, industry, infrastructure,
real estate -- billed by the hour: 17,179 employees, utilisation 72.9 %.
The customer returns because the next project needs the people who know the
plant or the grid. Order backlog 22.1 bn (20.4), ~10 months of sales.

## The reading

- **Organic growth negative**: -1.5 % Q3, -3.8 % Jan-Sep 2026, after the
  capacity cuts of the restructuring completed in Q2 2026. Total growth comes
  from AMC (bought Q2 2026, 628 m paid in the nine months).
- **Margin**: EBITA ex IAC 7.0 % Jan-Sep (6.8). The 10 % target for 2028 is
  above anything in the last nine years (best 8.4 %, 2018).
- **Net debt ex IFRS 16**: 4,843 at 30 Sep 2026 (3,904 at year end), 3.0x

- **Shares**: 113,251,741 (p.12). The vendor's count was about 4 % lower,
