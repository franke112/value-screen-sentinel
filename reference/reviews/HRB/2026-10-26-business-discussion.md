**The session's reading, as given to the owner:**

1. **HRB's low P/E is the market pricing structural risk.** That risk is
   free government filing, AI, and DIY software taking the customer.
   HRB has sat at about 8-12x for years. CENTA's risks are ordinary
   operating risks: weather, retailer pressure, debt.
2. **Part of HRB's EPS growth is buybacks.** About 8% of the share count
   was retired in FY2026, so EPS growth runs ahead of business growth.
3. **The gate exists because a tax-season report shows whether the
   clients stay.** No off-season report can show it.
4. **The discount to fv_base is about the same for both.** What differs
   is how far each fair value can be trusted.

## What others say (read 2026-10-26; web sources, UNVERIFIED)

### IRS Direct File was ended for the 2026 filing season

- Reported November 2025: the administration confirmed that Direct File
  would not be offered. Treasury Secretary Bessent, also acting as IRS
  commissioner, said usage was low and that the private sector can do
  better. The program began as a 2024 pilot funded through the IRA.
- The reading was **not** checked for whether the program returns for the
  2027 season.
- **This bears directly on the gate's own reading list**, which includes
  "any return of free government filing". The biggest named threat is
  absent for now. It is political, so it could come back with a change
  of administration.
- Sources:
  [CPA Practice Advisor](https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2025/11/05/irs-says-direct-file-wont-be-around-for-2026-filing-season/172448/),

## Why we and the street land so far apart

The street's targets of about 42-47 assume that earnings shrink, so they
price close to our bear case of -4%. Our base case is 2%. The removal of
Direct File favours our side. Revenue guidance of only +4-5% and the
share-loss narrative favour theirs.

The question the gate asks in May 2027 is the one that decides this:
assisted client volumes against price increases, and paid DIY customer
counts.
