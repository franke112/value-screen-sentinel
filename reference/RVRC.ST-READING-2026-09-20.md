## 1. THE FY2026 SHEET DESCRIBES THE PRE-ACQUISITION ENTITY

Every figure in the sheet of 2026-09-20 — net sales 2,018, EBIT 422, EBIT
margin 20.9%, operating cash flow 435, net cash 328, no interest-bearing
debt — is **standalone RevolutionRace for the year ended 2026-06-30**, from
`sources/RVRC.ST_2026-FY_financial-statement-release_2026-08-11_en_a0.pdf`.

**That is not the company that exists today.** ICANIWILL AB was acquired
after the year end and **closed on 2026-07-10**
(`RVRC.ST_2027-Q1_other-information-...-2026-07-10_en_a0.pdf`): 90.1% of the
shares, SEK 700 m enterprise value at closing, up to SEK 175 m of earn-out
on calendar-2026 and calendar-2027 EBIT targets, funded with about SEK 467 m
of cash and facilities plus 2,558,887 treasury shares at SEK 58.62, with the
revolving credit facility raised from SEK 600 m to SEK 900 m. ICIW's own
estimated LTM figures to 2026-06-30: net revenue SEK 470 m, adjusted EBIT
SEK 73 m. The group the company describes has annual sales of about SEK
2.5 bn and adjusted EBIT near SEK 500 m.

**So the FY2026 basis is a basis for a business that no longer stands
alone**, and any figure read from it carries that mark.

## 2. THE §4.3 BUYBACK FLAG CANNOT BE GREEN

`§4.3` green flag: *active buyback at depressed prices*. RVRC repurchased
SEK 201 m of shares in FY2026 and SEK 205 m in FY2025. **Those treasury
shares funded the acquisition**: 2,558,887 of them were transferred to
ICIW's sellers on 2026-07-10 at SEK 58.62, leaving 204,343 own shares.

**It is the same capital.** A buyback whose stock is handed over as purchase
consideration is not capital returned to shareholders at a depressed price;
it is acquisition currency accumulated in advance. **The flag cannot be
green for this name** (owner, 2026-09-20). Recorded as a ruling about the
evidence, not as a score.
