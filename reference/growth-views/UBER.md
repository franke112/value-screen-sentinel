# UBER — growth view

Set by the session under E141 (the owner, 2026-10-27: "låt warren ta besluten"), 2026-10-28, pre-registered under FRAMEWORK-EDITS E28 BEFORE any fair value was computed for this name.

Base case FCF growth, 10 years: 8%
Bear: 2%
Bull: 12%

## Reasons

Uber's history has no five years of positive owner earnings, and its revenue was reported net and gross across a change of presentation, so E144 cannot derive a rate and the view is the session's. Gross bookings grew 24% in Q2 2026 and revenue about 18% in 2025, but a ten-year rate has to carry the fade from a young profit base to a mature network. Base 8%: the network keeps growing in the low teens for some years and slows to the low single digits, with margins roughly where they are. Bear 2%: autonomous fleets owned by others (Waymo, Tesla) sell rides direct and the network becomes one channel among several, holding volume but not take rate. Bull 12%: autonomy runs through Uber's demand network and the delivery and freight lines keep compounding.
