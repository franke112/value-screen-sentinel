## E96 was tested FIRST, and this view exists because the answer was no

The owner raised it himself: he had described the business as *olja, gas,
bensinmackar*, and E96 removes at step 0 any company whose revenue is a
world price it does not set. **The test is written up in full in
`reference/E96-TEST-MUSA-2026-09-04.md` and was done BEFORE these three
numbers were written.**

The short of it, in the filer's own words: *"We purchase fuel... at rates
that fluctuate with market prices... and we sell fuel to our customers at
prices that WE ESTABLISH DAILY"*, and *"Dramatic increases in oil prices
REDUCE retail gasoline gross margins"*. The world price is Murphy USA's
COST; a producer's exposure runs the other way. E96 names *"refining and
marketing margins that move against the crude price"* among the things it
expressly does not sweep in, and this is the marketing half of that
sentence standing alone.
