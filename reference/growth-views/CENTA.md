# CENTA — growth view

**Set by the owner, 2026-10-20.** Pre-registered under FRAMEWORK-EDITS
**E28**, before the fair value that is entered was struck.

Base case FCF growth, 10 years: 2%
Bear: -1%
Bull: 3.5%

## The reasoning, in the owner's own words

**TRANSCRIBED VERBATIM AND NOT TIDIED.** The reason, given before the rates
were adopted:

> ookej men detta är fortfarande ett stabilt bolag, djur foder kommer
> alltid behövas husdjur är ingenting som kommer försvinna, de har avtal
> med stora kedjor som wallmart etc.

The adoption, verbatim: "ja vi kör".

**Stated so a later reader knows:** the rates were PROPOSED BY THE SESSION,
and the owner adopted them. The session also corrected two points of the
reasoning before the owner adopted it, and the owner did not object:

- the company sells mainly pet SUPPLIES and consumables, not food;
- about 40% of sales is Garden, which depends on the weather;
- the retailers (top five 54% of sales) are both the moat and the margin
  pressure.

The session's grounds for the proposal:
- **base 2%:** about inflation, with stable shelf space and retailer
  pressure on margin;
- **bear -1%:** weak garden years and price pressure;
- **bull 3.5%:** TRIXIE gives a foothold in Europe, plus margin
  improvement.

## Provenance and ordering, stated

**ORDERING DEPARTS FROM E28's INTENT, AND THAT IS RECORDED HERE.** Before
the owner adopted these rates, the session showed fair values on them: a
dry-run on 2/-1/3.5 gave base 61.83, bear 48.69 and bull 69.64 against
35.79. It also gave an adjusted estimate of 52-55 for the TRIXIE net debt
and tariff refunds.

What was in front of the owner:
- **History:** the FY2021-FY2025 store, with FY2022 FCF negative from the
  pandemic inventory build.
- **The nine months to June 2026:** operating cash flow 207.6m against
  149.8m.
- **Corporate events:** the TRIXIE agreement (up to EUR 400m, closing H1
  FY2027) and the 2023 CEO exit.
- **Guidance:** cut in 2022 and 2023, raised in 2025-2026.
- **Clean checks:** no auditor change, no restatement, no securities suit,
  no recall 2022-2026.
- **Tariff refunds** (IEEPA) inside FY2026.
