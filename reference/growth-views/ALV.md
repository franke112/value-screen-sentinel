**Set by the owner, 2026-10-20.** Pre-registered under FRAMEWORK-EDITS
**E28**, before the fair value that is entered was struck.

Base case FCF growth, 10 years: 3%
Bear: 1%
Bull: 5%

## The reasoning, in the owner's own words

**TRANSCRIBED VERBATIM AND NOT TIDIED.**

> 1, 3, 5 e bra på autoliv kör på det

**Stated so a later reader knows:** the rates were PROPOSED BY THE SESSION.
The owner had asked it to choose the best name and value it without him
("gör egen research o allt ... gör allt som behövs utan mig"). The owner
ADOPTED them, and the session's reasoning with them:

- airbags and seatbelts go into every new car, at about 40% of the world
  market;
- more content per vehicle gives growth even with flat light-vehicle
  production;
- base 3% is roughly inflation plus a little content gain;
- bear 1% is a weak auto cycle, tariffs, and price pressure from Chinese
  OEMs;
- bull 5% is content growth plus Asian volume.

The owner gave no reason beyond adopting these.

## Provenance and ordering, stated

**ORDERING DEPARTS FROM E28's INTENT, AND THAT IS RECORDED HERE.** Before
the owner chose, the session had already shown fair values on these same
rates: a dry-run on its own 1/3/5 gave base 134.34, bear 112.88 and bull
159.37 against a close of 116.49, with g\* 1.36%. So the view was not set
blind to the value. It was set by adopting the session's proposal, and the
values shown were computed on that proposal.

The facts in front of the owner when choosing:
- FY2025 FCF0 of $793m;
- FY2026 guidance of operating cash flow about $1.2bn, capex under 5% of
  sales, and organic growth about 0%;
- buybacks of $300-500m in 2026;
- the Q3 2026 report due 2026-10-23.
