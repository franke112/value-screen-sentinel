## The three rates, in the owner's words as recorded on 2026-08-27

**g_base 3%:** price and product levers keep working, forecourt count
stabilises; below the 8.2% four-year Trade CAGR because that period looks
over — FY2027 guidance is £395-415m operating profit against £392.7m
delivered, and forecourts guided 1-2% lower.

**g_bear 0%:** forecourt attrition continues at roughly the rate the
annual price rise adds, netting to nothing.

*This is NOT the company's own downside.* Viability scenario 3 (Annual
Report and Financial Statements 2026, p.52) models losing approximately
10% of retailers and a c.25% fall in Trade revenue over two years, which
is WORSE than g_bear 0% above. g_bear is the owner's stated view, recorded
as given; the company's own more severe scenario is named here so a later
reader sees both and does not mistake one for the other.

**g_bull 6%:** retailers return, Deal Builder monetises, pricing power
intact.
