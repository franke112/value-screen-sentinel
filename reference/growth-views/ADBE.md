## The thesis

> **Adobe owned creative software because professionals had no alternative. The
> dominance came from lock-in, not from the products improving, and the revenue
> comes from raising the price of software the user can never own.**

## Two things that sharpen it

> **Cancellation is made deliberately hard and the fines for that are cheaper
> than the revenue it protects — a company whose product is winning does not
> need to make leaving difficult.**

> **And I cannot name a revolutionary product from them in five years; Photoshop
> and Premiere have barely changed in ten. Firefly is incremental and is not the
> best AI, so their own AI cannibalises their own product without leading.**

## Why this is not simply another Oracle

> **AI now lets people create without them. That is the alternative that did not
> exist before, and it is why this is not simply another Oracle — a company can
> coast on lock-in for decades when customers have nowhere to go.**

*The distinction is the load-bearing part of the verdict. The claim is not that
lock-in is fragile in general — it is that this particular lock-in has just
acquired an exit that it did not have.*

---

## RE-ENTRY CONDITION

> **Deploying the capital into something genuinely different, as Oracle did with
> cloud rather than staying in its own category. Buying competitors inside
> creative software is not that. I see no sign of it today. A credible move of
> that kind reopens the name.**

**This is a dated, falsifiable condition and not a standing exclusion.** It names
what would change the answer, and it names what would *not* — an acquisition
inside creative software does not count, however large.

*Consistent with FRAMEWORK-EDITS **E27**: ADBE's exclusion is answered by an
information event, never by a price level.*

## What would show me wrong

The owner's own falsifiers, recorded so that a later reader can check the verdict
against them rather than against a memory of it:

1. **Subscriber COUNT growing rather than revenue per subscriber.** *This is the
   sharpest of the three: it tests the thesis directly. If the money is coming
   from more customers rather than from a higher price on the same customers,
   the central claim is wrong.*
2. **Churn stable or falling, if disclosed.**
3. **The company answering AI competition credibly rather than claiming it as an
   opportunity.**

**None of the three has been checked.** They are checks to run, not checks that
were run.
