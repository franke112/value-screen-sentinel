# SYNSAM.ST — growth view
Written 2026-08-25, before implied growth was solved.

Base case FCF growth, 10 years: 2.5%
Because: revenue from the subscription is booked upfront and the
receivable factored, so FCF growth needs rising NEW contract volume,
not a stable base. Gross customer inflow has fallen for twenty
quarters. Growth converges on price per customer, not volume.

Bear: 1%
Because: Denmark stays structurally impaired after the credit
legislation, competitors have copied the subscription, and Sweden at
~35-40% share has little left to take.

Bull: 5%
Because: Finland is early, growing 18-25% a quarter, and the market
leader was just sold; the store programme still has runway and payback
is down toward 2.5 years.
