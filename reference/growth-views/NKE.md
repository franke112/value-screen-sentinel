# NKE — growth view
Written 2026-08-25, before implied growth was solved.

Base case FCF growth, 10 years: 3%
Because: Nike is not going away, but the volume does not come back.
They let too many competitors into the category — On, Hoka and Brooks
went from under 10% share to about 17%, and Anta passed them in China.
They also diluted their own franchises by overselling Air Force 1,
Air Jordan 1 and Dunk, and the cultural position that came with them
is gone until something proves otherwise. Growth is roughly price,
with a little optimism for the running business.

Bear: 0%
Because: the lifestyle loss is permanent and China never recovers.
China is down about 30% in five years on a market that grew, and the
cause is described as structural rather than cyclical.

Bull: 5%
Because: Hill's rebuild works — gross margin returns from 40% toward
the historical mid-40s, running keeps its double-digit growth, and
China stops bleeding. Off a depressed base that compounds fast.
