## The three rates, in the owner's words as recorded on 2026-09-19

**g_base 3%:** organic growth was 2% in H1 2026 and 3% in Q2, with recurring operating margin held at 22.5%. The luxury boom has normalised, not reversed. 3% is roughly current run-rate, no recovery assumed.

**g_bear 0%:** FY2025 flows are the basis, and a flat decade for a discretionary, China- and tourism-exposed group is a real case. Reported revenue still fell 3% in H1 on currency.

**g_bull 6%:** what a normal luxury cycle looks like with Fashion & Leather recovering and Watches & Jewellery already at 9%. Not the post-COVID double digits; I don't underwrite those.

These are my rates, not a forecast. Re-register after FY2026 results.
