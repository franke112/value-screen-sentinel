# E140 re-valuation of every struck name, 2026-10-27

This applies ruling E140 of 2026-10-27 (FRAMEWORK-EDITS). It changes three
things:

- **FCF0** is the mean of the last three fiscal years, with operating
  working capital taken out.
- **The interest add-back** is taken after tax.
- **Every deal after the basis** enters the bridge, from
  `reference/events/<TICKER>.yaml`.

Everything else is the run record's own: the growth view, the 9.5% rate,
2.5% terminal growth, ten years, the other bridge legs and the divisor.

US names were computed by `tools/normalize_e140.py` from SEC XBRL. The
working-capital tags used for each year were printed and read in full. The
non-US names were read from the cash-flow statements by subagent. Every
quote was checked by machine against the pdftotext output, and the per-year
arithmetic is in the session log.

**Nothing here writes `fv_base`. Writing it is the owner's act.**

The base value is shown as a range: the low end has deals at the HIGH end of
their price range, the high end at the LOW end. Prices are the close of
2026-10-26 (GBX names converted to GBP).
