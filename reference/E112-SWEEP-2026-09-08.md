# E112 applied across every stored name — 2026-09-08

**THE RULE.** §3 Gate 3's interest-coverage numerator is **operating income
as the accounts state it, impairments included.** Nothing is added back.

**NO STORED NAME CHANGES SIDE.** The reasoning is short and it bounds the
whole sweep: not adjusting can only make the numerator **smaller**, so this
rule can only ever move a name **down** through the 5× line and never up. **A
name above 5× on stated operating income is above it on any adjusted basis
too** — so only a name already *below* 5× could differ, and there is one.

**SYNSAM.ST is the only name below the limit, and it does NOT change side.**
Its interim reports carry **no impairment inside EBIT** — the word appears
once in the Q2 2026 report, in the definition of an adjusted measure, and the
forty hits in the FY2025 annual are accounting policy and *"visual and
hearing impairment"*, which is what the business treats. It fails on ordinary
trading, and it fails under either reading.

## Twenty stores cannot form it, and for two different reasons

**Eighteen carry no `operating_income` at all.** That is not a gap in those
stores: **the SEC XBRL path deliberately writes none** (`OMITTED_FIELDS`),
because operating income is not a single unambiguous tagged concept across
filers. Every sec-xbrl store is in this group — ACN, AOS, CROX, CTSH, DECK,
EXE, GDDY, GNTX, LII, LOPE, MUSA, NKE, NVR, SAP.DE, ULTA — with LIAB.ST and
LUG.ST beside them. For all of them Gate 3's coverage limb is **DATA
MISSING**, before and after this ruling.

**Two have a NEGATIVE denominator: APN.L (−0) and RMV.L (−2,077).** Both earn
more interest than they pay. **A net finance income is not a coverage**, and
dividing by it produces a negative "coverage" that reads as catastrophic
failure for a company with no interest burden at all — so the reading is
**NOT MEANINGFUL**, which is a different answer from a failure. Coded that
way, with the reason on the figure.

## The one name the rule bites, and it is not in the table

**CROX**, whose 1.69× raised the question, is not in the sweep because its
store carries no `operating_income` — the figures come from the 10-K by hand:
income from operations **149,515** over interest expense **88,287**.

**That is the other side of the limit**, and it is exactly what E112 says it
gives up: *a company with one large genuine one-off will fail a limb it would
pass on any normalised basis.* Printed as information, never as the verdict.

**CROX was dropped the same day on Gate 2**, before the coverage limb
mattered.
