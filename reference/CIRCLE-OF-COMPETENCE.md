# The owner's circle of competence — A READING NOTE, NOT A FILTER

**Recorded 2026-09-20, in the owner's own terms.**

**THIS IS NOT A SCREENER EXCLUSION AND MUST NEVER BECOME ONE.** E51
(pharmaceuticals and biotech) and E96 (a revenue that is a world price the
company does not set) remove names at step 0, with the reason recorded, and
those are rules about what **this framework can value**. This note is a
different thing: it is about **what the owner can judge**, and it **decides
which names are worth an evening, not which names exist**. A name outside
the circle is still screened, still ranked, still readable, and may still be
bought — it simply starts by owing an explanation of why the evening is
worth spending.

## What the owner can judge

From **17 years in IT infrastructure and 10+ as a consultant**:

- **IT services and consulting**
- **Enterprise networking and security**
- **Systems software and enterprise platforms**

And specifically, the three questions inside those industries that he can
answer from experience rather than from reading:

1. **Whether a vendor's position is defensible.**
2. **How customers get locked in.**
3. **Whether AI displaces billable hours.**

That third one is not a general opinion about AI. It is the particular
question of whether work that was billed by the hour stops being billed,
which is the live question on CTSH and ACN and the fear priced into AUTO.L.

## What the owner cannot judge

**Capital-intensity cycles he has no view on.** Named explicitly:

- **Semiconductor capacity and memory pricing** — Micron, Intel and their
  kind. **However familiar the products are.** Knowing what a DRAM module
  does is not a view on the capacity cycle that sets its price, and the
  familiarity is exactly what makes this one worth writing down.
- **Oil and gas** — already a framework exclusion under E96, for a different
  reason (the price is a world price). It is outside the circle as well.
- **Homebuilders** — the figures lag the housing cycle by about a year, so
  the name is a view on mortgage rates and affordability whether or not it
  is held as one. See PHM's entry, 2026-09-20.

## How to use it

- **It is read BEFORE the evening is spent**, not after the work is done.
- **A name outside the circle is not rejected**; it is a name whose price
  must justify the cost of learning the cycle it turns on. PHM's entry
  records the shape of that test: the market paid for about 1.4% growth
  while the leading indicators deteriorated faster than that, so the price
  did not compensate for acquiring the view.
- **Nothing here overrides a ruling**, and nothing here adds one. It changes
  no gate, no score, no tier and no verdict.
