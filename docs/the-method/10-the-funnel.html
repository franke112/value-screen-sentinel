The funnel: six statuses

The claimEvery company the method has touched is in exactly one of six states, and only a person moves it from one to another.

About 9 minutes · the builder's route.

A company that the method has noticed is always in exactly one state, and the state is written in the owner's watchlist file. The six states, in the tool's own spellings, are INTAKE, PIPELINE, WATCH-GATED, WATCH-PRICED, HELD and DROPPED.11The status list in vss/rules.py. A retired seventh spelling, plain WATCH, is refused by the code with a message pointing at the ruling that split it. The order below is the order a name would travel if everything went well, which it almost never does.

INTAKE: read, not watched

What happens
A name is being studied. Its filings are read, figures are entered and checked, a growth view may be written. Nothing about it is watched yet.
The tool does
Fetches its filings on request, stores its figures with provenance, reports what is verified and what is missing. It does not watch the price.
The owner does
Reads. Decides whether to enter the name into the process at all.
Falls out when
The owner decides it is not worth entering. Nothing automatic happens here.

This state exists because of a deadlock the method created for itself: the growth view has to be written before the value is solved, but entering a name used to stamp the date and freeze a gate, so a name could not be studied without being committed to. INTAKE is "read, not watched", and it carries no entry stamp.22Ruling E111, 2026-09-04, resolving the deadlock ruling E109 had created. The code refuses an INTAKE entry that carries any of the fields that only an entered name may have.

PIPELINE: entered

What happens
The name has been entered. The date of entry is stamped and frozen. The old "how far has it fallen" gate is gone (E161), so no price test is tied to that day.
The tool does
Watches for the company's own disclosures: periodic reports, changes to its forecast, profit warnings. Nothing else. Leadership changes, buybacks, deals and shareholder notices are filtered out, on the owner's test: would this make me redo the valuation?
The owner does
Works through the gates, the hard kills, the reading, the growth view, the valuation.
Falls out when
A gate fails on something other than price (to WATCH-GATED), a hard kill or a class D reason is found (to DROPPED), or the business turns out to be outside the circle (to DROPPED).

Because entry stamps a date and that stamp cannot be undone by re-running anything, the scheduled screen is forbidden from entering a name. It may propose. Only a person enters: the owner, or the session at his word.33Ruling E93: 'entering a PIPELINE name stamps dd_at_entry and peak_date and E12 freezes Gate 1 at that moment — and unlike a fair value, that is not undone by re-striking.' The flag that would allow it is absent from the scheduled unit and must stay absent.

WATCH-GATED: waiting for a fact

What happens
The name failed a gate on something that was not its price. It waits.
The tool does
Watches its disclosures, as for PIPELINE. Does not watch its price, because no price would change the answer.
The owner does
Nothing, until a named new fact arrives. Then re-reads.
Falls out when
The owner decides the fact will never come (to DROPPED), or a new fact reopens the gate (back to PIPELINE).

WATCH-PRICED: waiting for a price

What happens
The reading is done, the value is struck, and a cushion price is computed as information. The price is above it. The name waits for the price alone.
The tool does
Every night, the queue compares the settled closing price with the look line, the price at which the name is worth valuing properly again, and with the cushion price. It tells the owner when either is crossed: TITTA for the look line, UNDER MBP for the cushion price. Neither says buy. The cushion price is computed or there is none: a level typed in by hand for a name never valued is a reminder, and the name is not buyable at it.
The owner does
Waits. Re-values on the company's next report.
Falls out when
The owner buys (to HELD), or a new report changes the reading (back to PIPELINE, or to DROPPED).

HELD: owned

What happens
The owner has bought. The exit was written down before buying: a bull value, a sell line set by hand, or the condition that breaks the thesis. "None" is accepted for a stop.
The tool does
Every night, checks the price against the sell line and the bull value, and sends SÄLJ when one is reached. A held name with a blank exit field is a blocker, reported before anything else.
The owner does
Re-values on every report. Sells at the sell line, at the bull value, or when the reason for buying is gone.
Falls out when
The owner sells. The sale is recorded and measured against the market from its own date.

Changed, November 2026. The price stop is gone (ruling E162): no stop level, with or without buying back, helped in the tests, and with trading costs every one cost money. The owner still writes the exit down before buying, but it is written as stop_price: none, a decision and not a blank; a blank still blocks. Selling because the reason for owning is gone stays. See chapter 13.

DROPPED: refused

What happens
The name has been refused: a hard kill, a class D reason, outside the circle, or the owner's judgement.
The tool does
Does not watch it at all. Records the refusal in the shadow book with the price of the day and one line of reason (chapter 12).
The owner does
Nothing. Reads the shadow book once a year.
Falls out when
It does not. A dropped name can be re-entered at INTAKE by the owner, as a new decision, with a new record.

The thing to notice

Every arrow between the boxes is a human act: the owner's, or, since E141 (2026-10-27), the Claude Code session's on his behalf. The tool measures where a name stands, reports it, and points when something crosses a line. The scheduled machine never moves a name. If nobody acts, nothing moves. That is a design decision, and chapter 14 says why.

The master list

One more file ties the statuses together. reports/HUVUDLISTA.md is the master list, built by tools/master_list.py and rebuilt every night. It holds every company ever looked at, in groups: owned, candidates, watched, to review, and no. Each row has the price, the bear, base and bull values, the kind of value, the reason, and the next dated event. Each Claude Code session opens with its short version, so no session starts by guessing where things stand.

The exact rules

Statuses: vss/rules.py. INTAKE: ruling E111. PIPELINE entry stamps and the frozen gate: rulings E12 and E93. The two waiting lists: ruling E27. The disclosure filter: vss/watch.py. The nightly price comparison: vss/runner.py, vss/rules.py. Sales: ruling B45, vss/sales.py. Refusals: ruling E114, vss/shadowbook.py.

CENTA at this step

CENTA entered as PIPELINE on 20 October and became HELD the same day. It is still HELD: two shares are left. In the master list of 9 November it stood under 'owned', at 41.84 dollars against a base value of 40.19. A status says where a company stands in the process. It does not say the process was followed.

Try it
.venv/bin/python tools/master_list.py --short

Every company looked at, in five groups, with the cheapest to review first. From the repository's root, after the install in the manual.

In three lines
  1. Six statuses: in the pipeline, read but not watched, watched for price, watched behind a gate, held, dropped.
  2. The tool reports where a name stands and points when something crosses a line. It never moves a name.
  3. The master list puts all of them on one page, rebuilt every night.
