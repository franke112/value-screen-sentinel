## 5. Capital allocation

**THE 2025 RETURN, AS STATED** — *FY2025 results, verbatim:*

```
· £219.7m of surplus cash returned to shareholders through share buybacks and dividends (2024: £181.7m), an increase of
21% year on year. 21.4m shares (3% of outstanding share capital) purchased and cancelled (2024: 18.8m, 2%)
· Final dividend up 8% to 6.59p per share (2024: 6.10p). Total dividend up 9% to 10.64p (2024: 9.8p)
```

**THE SHARE COUNT IS BEING SHRUNK, NOT JUST SUPPORTED.** 21.4 million shares purchased **and cancelled** in 2025 — 3 percent of outstanding share capital — after 18.8 million (2 percent) in 2024. That is the mechanical part of the gap between basic EPS **+15%** and revenue **+9%**.

**WHAT WAS ANNOUNCED SINCE.** A **£90m buyback** announced 2026-02-27, *"to be completed by 31 July 2026"*; a final dividend of *"c.£50m"* paid May 2026. Then, on 2026-07-31, **"Over £400m capital returns expected in next 12 months (to July 2027), including approximately £330m of increased share buybacks"**.

**AND THE FUNDING CHANGED WITH IT.** The same sentence states the increased buyback is *"to be funded by a new £200m revolving credit facility alongside cash from operations"*. **A company that returned £219.7m of surplus cash in 2025 is proposing to return over £400m in the following twelve months with a new credit facility beside the cash flow.** Both halves are the issuer's own words; the observation is arithmetic and nothing more is claimed.
