## 8. What this could NOT answer

**From the filings.**

- **MARIMEKKO'S CASH FLOW STATEMENT PRINTS NO STANDALONE QUARTER**, and that is the largest single finding of this intake. The income statement gives 4–6/2026 and the balance sheet is a point in time, but the cash flow statement carries only cumulative columns — 1–6/2026, 1–6/2025, 1–12/2025 — in all four interim documents. **A twelve-month basis ending 2026-06-30 could only be reached by subtracting one cumulative column from another, which is derivation and is not done.** So the store's basis is the year as filed, **annual FY2025 ending 2025-12-31**, which is two quarters staler — and the two quarters it excludes are the ones in which Finland fell 4% and the margin ran at 12.2%.

E101's second construction check therefore has no stated comparator, though the ratios reconcile against the store's own bridge to within a cent.
- **No gross profit line.** Costs are presented by nature, so E6's quality numerator does not exist for this name.
- **`Tangible assets 32.4` includes right-of-use assets** under IFRS 16 and no note read here splits owned from leased, so `net_ppe` carries a caveat rather than a clean figure.
- **No competitor is named anywhere in the five documents.**
- **No store-count-by-country, no like-for-like sales, no average selling price and no gross margin percentage.** For a retailer, the absence of a like-for-like figure is the notable one: the reader cannot separate new-store growth from same-store growth in any market area.
