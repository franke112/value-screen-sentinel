- 52-week closing high **72.90** on 2025-08-22; 52-week closing low **42.55** on 2026-06-22; last close **48.85** on 2026-09-04.
- The high of the last two years is **81.20** on 2025-01-24.
- Peak (2025-08-22) to trough (2026-06-22): **−30.35, or −41.6%**, over about 210 trading days.

## 8. What this could NOT answer

**From the filings.**

- **No competitor is named anywhere in the five documents.** The word "competitive" appears; a competitor does not. Any peer comparison has to come from outside the filings, and section 2's is web-sourced.
- **Bouvet attaches no figure of any kind to AI.** Across 2 403 employees, five documents and a year of reporting there is no AI revenue, no AI cost, no AI productivity measure, and no statement connecting AI to the hourly rate, the billing ratio, the headcount or the revenue line. The bridge in section 3a is the only quantitative series that bears on the question, and the issuer attributes its movements to holidays, project progress and sick leave.
- **The billing ratio itself is never printed as a level.** Only the year-on-year change in percentage points is given, so the reader cannot tell whether utilisation is falling from a high base or a low one, and cannot compare it to any peer.
