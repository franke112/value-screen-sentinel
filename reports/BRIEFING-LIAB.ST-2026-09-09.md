## 8. What this could NOT answer

Named in one place, which is the part the reading consumes.

1. **Why the shares fell on 2026-02-12 and again on 2026-02-13** — together −11.5 percent over two sessions, on the full-year report. No dated source was returned and none is printed. This is the largest unexplained move in the window and the only one of the five largest with no reason attached.
2. **Swegon's figures.** Lindab names exactly two competitors and only one of them publishes. Swegon is inside Investment AB Latour and files no standalone report on the Nasdaq feed, so the comparison in section 2 has one peer and not two.
3. **What Connaught Ventilation Supplies cost.** The acquisition is announced in the Q2 2026 report with no consideration, no revenue and no employee count. It is the newest acquisition and the least documented.
4. **Whether the insider purchases were open-market or pre-scheduled.** The Swedish register carries no field that answers it (section 7). A reader who wants that distinction cannot get it from this source.
5. **The full board.** The extraction of *p.51* truncates the director list after five names; two further directors are known only because they appear in the insider register. Board tenure, other directorships and committee membership were not transcribed.
6. **Order book, backlog or forward volume.** Lindab discloses none in any of the five documents, and the CEO comments are the only forward-looking material in them.
7. **Segment-level cash flow and capital employed.** The reports give segment net sales, operating profit, margin and employees; they do not split cash flow, capital employed or net debt by segment, so the SEK 2,524 m R12M Profile Systems business cannot be separated from the Group's balance sheet.
8. **What the 2026 AGM resolved.** The annual report was signed 2026-04-07 and states only that the meeting *"will be held on May 12"*; no notice, minutes or resolution from that meeting is in `sources/`, so the 2026 dividend actually paid, and any new repurchase authorisation, are unread.
